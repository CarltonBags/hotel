# Payments provider for EU/DACH hotels (Stripe vs Adyen vs Mollie vs Unzer)

Research date: 2026-09-24. Scope: multi-tenant SaaS PMS, EU-wide with Germany/AT/CH first, hotels up to ~400 rooms. Needs: booking-engine online payments, front-desk card-present terminals, hotel-style pre-auth with incremental auth and delayed capture, payouts to each hotel (platform model), OTA virtual-card (VCC) handling, refunds, fees.

All claims cite official provider documentation (see Sources). Claims that could not be confirmed from a primary source are marked **UNVERIFIED**.

## Recommendation: Stripe (Connect + Terminal), with Adyen as the enterprise fallback

**Recommendation: Stripe.** Reasoning:

1. **Hotel-specific card features are documented end to end, both online and card-present.** Stripe publishes the exact hold windows (7-day default, 30-day extended for hotel/lodging MCCs), incremental authorization (max 10 increments, MIT after an initial 3DS), multicapture (up to 50 partial captures), and the `capture_before` field that tells the PMS when a hold dies [S1][S2][S3][S4]. The same primitives exist on Terminal (`request_extended_authorization`, `request_incremental_authorization_support`) [S8][S9]. Adyen matches this functionally (PreAuth + adjust authorisation, 30-day Visa/MC lodging validity) [A1][A3], but Mollie caps automatic capture at 7 days and documents no incremental auth [M1], and Unzer's docs stop at "at least 7 days" with no adjust-auth API [U2].
2. **Terminals are generally available in DE, AT and CH under one API**, including girocard in Germany [S7]. Adyen also covers all three [A4]. Mollie's terminal launched only in BE/DE/NL [M3] and Unzer publishes no terminal API [U5].
3. **Platform model fits a SaaS PMS.** Connect direct charges are Stripe's own recommendation for SaaS platforms; hosted/embedded onboarding does KYC; Stripe can carry negative-balance risk; Terminal readers can be owned by the connected hotel account [S14][S15][S12]. Adyen for Platforms is comparable but access is gated by a sales assessment and a minimum invoice [A11][A14]; Mollie Connect caps application fees (Orders API max €2.00) [M10]; Unzer's marketplace product has no public developer docs [U8].
4. **Self-serve, transparent list pricing** with published Connect fees [S16][S17]. Adyen's IC++ will be cheaper at volume but requires a negotiated contract.

**Caveats that shape the decision:**
- Stripe's extended auth, incremental auth, multicapture and overcapture are labelled IC+ features; a platform on blended pricing must request access from Stripe Support before relying on them [S2][S3][S4][S5]. Confirm this in writing before launch.
- Adyen is the right fallback (or second integration) once a large chain demands IC++ pricing or Adyen-only features; keep the folio's payment abstraction provider-agnostic.

## Constraints on folio and booking-engine flows

- **Every card hold has an expiry the folio must track.** Store `capture_before` per authorization; default online window is 7 days (Visa MIT 5 days), Terminal 2 days (Visa 5) [S1][S9]. Request `request_extended_authorization=if_available` on both online and card-present PaymentIntents; only trust `extended_authorization.status == enabled` [S2]. Stays longer than the confirmed window need a scheduled re-authorization job (cancel + new auth or new PI) before expiry.
- **Extended 30-day windows depend on the hotel's MCC.** Each connected account must carry MCC 7011 (hotels/lodging); Stripe auto-assigns MCCs but the platform can set `configuration.merchant.mcc` / `business_profile.mcc` [S19][S9]. Visa/Amex/Discover extended auth is lodging-only; Amex requires capture no later than end of stay [S2].
- **Incremental auth does not extend the window and is capped.** Max 10 increments per PaymentIntent; each increment ≤ max(USD 500 equiv., 500% of prior amount); cannot increment after a partial multicapture [S3]. The folio should aggregate incidentals and increment in batches, and fall back to a fresh authorization when limits are hit.
- **SCA sequencing for incidentals.** Booking-engine deposits/guarantees must run 3DS on the first authorization with `setup_future_usage=off_session`; later increments and post-checkout charges are MITs with `off_session=true` and must be covered by consent text shown at booking (frequency, how amount is determined) [S3][S10]. Overcapture is not an option in the EEA — authenticate the highest expected amount instead [S5].
- **Capture semantics.** A single capture below the authorized amount releases the rest; if you need several partial captures (e.g. deposit, then balance), request `request_multicapture=if_available` on online cards; Terminal charges do not support multicapture [S4][S1]. Uncaptured PaymentIntents cannot be refunded, only cancelled [S18].
- **SEPA Direct Debit is not a hold instrument.** No manual capture, €10,000 per-transaction limit, T+6 settlement, 8-week no-reason chargeback and 13-month unauthorised window, disputes cannot be contested [S6]. Use SEPA DD only for prepayments/invoices with a ≥6-business-day "not final" state in the folio; never for check-in guarantees.
- **Card-present at the front desk.** Create Terminal PaymentIntents with `capture_method=manual`, `request_extended_authorization=true` and `request_incremental_authorization_support=true` (the reader then shows "Pre-authorization"); check `incremental_authorization_supported` after confirm [S8][S9]. Readers and locations belong to the hotel's connected account (`Stripe-Account` header) for direct charges [S12].
- **OTA virtual cards are keyed-in MOTO transactions.** They are card-not-present and SCA-exempt; EEA accounts are auto-flagged MOTO for manually entered cards [S11]. Terminal MOTO entry needs Stripe Support enablement and is limited to S700/S710/WisePOS E [S10]. Card-present liability shift and Terminal pricing do not apply to MOTO [S10]. The folio should record VCC payments as a distinct tender type (charge on/after the OTA's activation date, often full capture at check-in/out, no incremental auth expected).
- **Refunds and disputes hit the hotel's balance under direct charges.** Refunds are limited to the original payment method and captured amount; SEPA refunds within 180 days; original processing fees are not returned [S18][S6][S14]. The folio needs a "refund pending insufficient balance" state, because Stripe holds card refunds when the connected balance is negative [S18].
- **Payout model.** With direct charges funds settle in the hotel's country and account; the platform takes `application_fee_amount`; platform-priced accounts cost €2/active account/month plus payout fees [S14][S16][S17]. Night-audit reconciliation should key on Stripe balance transactions per connected account, not on the platform account.
