## 1. Federal rule (in force)

### 1.1 Statute and ordinance

- **AIG Art. 16** (SR 142.20, Stand 1 Jan 2025), "Meldepflicht bei gewerbsmässiger Beherbergung": *"Wer Ausländerinnen oder Ausländer gewerbsmässig beherbergt, muss sie der zuständigen kantonalen Behörde melden."* (Whoever accommodates foreign nationals commercially must report them to the competent cantonal authority.) [F1]
- **VZAE Art. 18** (SR 142.201, Stand 1 Jan 2025), "Meldeverfahren bei gewerbsmässiger Beherbergung": [F2]
  - Abs. 1: *"Wer eine Ausländerin oder einen Ausländer gegen Entgelt beherbergt, ist verpflichtet, einen Meldeschein gemäss den Angaben im Ausweispapier auszufüllen und diesen von der beherbergten Person unterschreiben zu lassen. Die beherbergte Person muss ihre Ausweispapiere zu diesem Zweck vorlegen. Der Meldeschein ist der zuständigen kantonalen Behörde zu übermitteln."* (Whoever accommodates a foreign national for payment must fill in a registration form according to the details in the identity document and have it signed by the accommodated person. The accommodated person must present their identity documents for this purpose. The form is to be transmitted to the competent cantonal authority.)
  - Abs. 2: *"Bei Gruppen erfolgt die Meldung durch eine vom verantwortlichen Reiseleiter unterschriebene Liste."* (For groups the report is made by a list signed by the responsible tour leader.)
- The federal rule covers **foreign nationals only**. Swiss guests are covered, where at all, by cantonal law. The federal explanatory report of 26 Aug 2026 states: *"In zahlreichen Kantonen müssen auch Schweizer Gäste gemeldet werden. Diese Meldungen basieren auf kantonalem Recht."* (In numerous cantons Swiss guests must also be reported. These reports are based on cantonal law.) [F4, section 1.1.1]
- The origin is Art. 45 of the Schengen Convention (SDÜ/CISA), which Switzerland applies as an associated state. [F4, section 1.1.1]
- Enforcement belongs to the cantons: *"Der Vollzug des Meldewesens ist Aufgabe der Kantone. ... Hauptnutzende sind die kantonalen Polizeien, welche die Meldescheine bei Fahndungen oder der Vermisstensuche prüfen."* (Enforcement is the task of the cantons. The main users are the cantonal police forces, which check the forms in manhunts and missing-person searches.) *"Der Bund erhebt selbst keine Daten und führt keine Kontrollen durch."* (The Confederation collects no data itself and performs no checks.) [F4, section 1.1.1]

### 1.2 Who is an accommodation provider

The criterion is payment, not regularity. The explanatory report: *"das einzige bestimmende Kriterium ist das «Entgelt» ... Somit sind Privatpersonen, die eine Online-Plattform ... (z. B. Airbnb, Wimdu, FeWo) nutzen, um ihre Wohnung gegen Entgelt zu vermieten, ebenfalls als «beherbergende Personen» zu betrachten und meldepflichtig"* (the only determining criterion is the payment; private persons renting out via online platforms are also accommodation providers and subject to the reporting duty). [F4, section 3]

### 1.3 Mandatory federal fields

The explanatory report lists the fields that federal law makes mandatory, citing the annex "Hotelmeldeschein" to SEM directive I. Ausländerbereich, number 3.1.3.2: [F4, section 3, on Art. 18 Abs. 1]

> *"Gemäss den bundesrechtlichen Vorgaben, müssen zwingend folgende Informationen erhoben und gemeldet werden: Name, Vorname, Geburtsdatum, Geburtsort, Staatsangehörigkeit, Angaben zur Begleitung durch Ehegatten und minderjährige Kinder, Ankunft und Abreisedatum sowie Art des Ausweispapieres und Ausweisnummer und Name des Beherbergungsbetriebs."*

English gloss, as a field list:

1. Surname
2. First name
3. Date of birth
4. Place of birth
5. Nationality
6. Accompanying spouse and minor children (details of accompaniment)
7. Arrival date
8. Departure date
9. Type of identity document
10. Identity document number
11. Name of the accommodation establishment

Note what is **not** on the federal list: home address, sex, profession, vehicle plate, signature image. Cantons add some of these (see section 3).

### 1.4 Signature under the law in force

- The ordinance in force says "unterschreiben zu lassen" with no form specified. The federal administration itself reads the current rule as requiring a **handwritten** signature: the report refers to *"Die in Artikel 45 Absatz 1 Buchstabe a SDÜ geforderte handschriftliche Unterschrift"* (the handwritten signature required by Art. 45 (1) (a) SDÜ) and explains that the amendment is needed precisely to allow an electronic form without signature. [F4, section 1.2]
- The same report observes existing practice without ruling on its validity: *"Teilweise wird auch bereits eine online-Unterschrift verlangt."* (In some cases an online signature is already requested.) [F4, section 1.2] Whether a signature drawn on a tablet satisfies VZAE Art. 18 today is not decided by any federal text found; it depends on the cantonal authority. **UNVERIFIED at federal level**; see per-canton findings.

### 1.5 Retention under federal law

Neither AIG Art. 16 nor VZAE Art. 18 sets a retention period for the form. [F1][F2] Retention is cantonal (section 3). SDÜ Art. 45 (1) (b) requires that the forms are kept available for or transmitted to the authorities, as paraphrased in the report: *"ist der Beherbergende verpflichtet, diese Daten für die zuständigen Behörden bereitzuhalten oder diesen zu übermitteln"*. [F4, section 2.1]

## 2. Amendment in consultation (state on 2026-09-29)

- **Opened** by the Bundesrat on 26 Aug 2026; **consultation runs until 27 Nov 2026**. [F3]
- **Trigger**: Motion Gmür-Schönenberger 21.4426 "Schluss mit dem Meldeschein-Chaos in der Beherbergung". [F4, Übersicht]
- **Status**: preliminary draft (Vorentwurf) only. The entry-into-force date is blank in the draft: *"Diese Verordnung tritt am … in Kraft."* [F5] Nothing is adopted. The text may change after the consultation.
- **Draft Art. 18 E-VZAE** (total revision of the article), verbatim: [F5]
  - Abs. 1: *"Wer eine Ausländerin oder einen Ausländer gegen Entgelt beherbergt, muss der zuständigen kantonalen Behörde einen Meldeschein mit den Angaben des Ausweispapiers der beherbergten Person übermitteln. Die beherbergte Person muss sich zu diesem Zweck bei Ankunft ausweisen. Die beherbergende Person muss überprüfen, ob die Daten im Meldeschein dem vorgelegten Ausweispapier entsprechen."* (... must transmit a form with the details of the identity document. The guest must identify themselves on arrival. The provider must check that the data on the form match the document presented.)
  - Abs. 2: *"Wird der Meldeschein in Papierform erstellt, so muss er von der beherbergten Person unterschrieben werden. Der Meldeschein kann auch elektronisch erstellt und ohne Unterschrift der beherbergten Person übermittelt werden."* (A paper form must be signed by the guest. The form may also be created electronically and transmitted without the guest's signature.)
  - Abs. 3: *"Bei Gruppen erfolgt die Meldung durch eine von der verantwortlichen Reiseleiterin oder vom verantwortlichen Reiseleiter unterschriebene Liste. Die Meldung kann auch elektronisch und ohne Unterschrift erfolgen, wenn sie die Kontaktdaten der Reiseleiterin oder des Reiseleiters enthält."* (Groups: signed list, or electronic without signature if it contains the tour leader's contact details.)
- **What replaces the signature**: the provider's check of the data against the presented document plus visual verification of the person. *"Die Überprüfung der Daten des Gastes bei der Ankunft mittels Abgleichs mit dem Ausweispapier sowie die Verifizierung dessen Identität mittels Augenscheins durch den Beherbergenden kann somit das eigenhändige Ausfüllen und das Unterschreiben des Meldescheins ersetzen"*. [F4, section 1.2] This maps directly onto the **ID Check** step already decided in ticket 11.
- **Who enters the data** is left open: *"Die Erfassung der Daten kann durch den Gast oder die beherbergende Person erfolgen."* The technical design *"ist der beherbergenden Person überlassen und richtet sich gegebenenfalls nach kantonalem Recht"* (is left to the provider and follows cantonal law where applicable). [F4, section 3, on Abs. 2]
- **EasyGov**: a procedure "Meldewesen für die Beherbergung" is to be built on EasyGov, **implementation planned for 2028**. It is a portal only; guest data flow only between provider and canton. Use is **voluntary**. Cantons with established systems keep them: *"In Kantonen mit bereits etablierten und gut funktionierenden digitalen Meldesystemen wie beispielsweise Basel-Stadt, Freiburg oder Tessin kann die Meldung der Gästedaten weiterhin mit den bestehenden Systemen erfolgen."* [F4, section 1.1.3 and section 3]
- **No interface standard exists today**. The report records that connecting hotel management systems is hard *"da keine Standards vorhanden sind"* (because no standards exist). [F4, section 1.2.1] No EasyGov API or data format has been published in the consultation documents. **Format of the future EasyGov channel: UNVERIFIED / not yet defined.**
- **Card payment path rejected for Switzerland**: the report notes that the German substitution of the signature by a card payment with strong customer authentication *"sich ... als wenig praxis-tauglich erwiesen hat und kaum genutzt wird"* (proved impractical and is hardly used). The Swiss draft does not contain a card path. [F4, section 1.2]
- **Consequence for cantonal law**: the amendment changes federal law for foreign guests only. Cantonal provisions that require a signature (for all guests or for Swiss guests) stay in force until each canton amends them. The report does not address this. **Whether each canton will drop its own signature requirement: unsettled.**

It is a government-commissioned survey of the cantons, mostly answered by the cantonal police forces, and is the only cross-canton source found. It is **self-reported survey data from 2024**, not statute; every row below was checked against cantonal primary sources where those could be retrieved (sections 3.1 to 3.8). [F6]

Caveat: the table was extracted from the PDF by column position; the row totals match the study's own counts (14, 16, 6, 11, 13), so the mapping is believed correct.

Other findings of the study that matter for the product:

- **Signature in practice**: *"Der Verzicht auf die Unterschrift wird in den Kantonen mit elektronischen Meldemöglichkeiten bereits praktiziert. Unterschrieben werden Meldescheine in Papierform."* (Waiving the signature is already practised in the cantons with electronic reporting. Paper forms are signed.) [F6, section 3.3.5] And: *"Gemäss den gesetzlichen Vorgaben ist eine handschriftliche Unterschrift des Gastes verpflichtend. Die kantonale Praxis kann davon abweichen und festlegen, ob ein Ausdruck des Meldescheins erstellt und dem Gast zur Unterschrift vorgelegt werden muss."* (By law a handwritten signature is mandatory. Cantonal practice may deviate and determine whether a printout must be presented for signature.) [F6, section 3.2.2] So current cantonal practice is ahead of the federal text; the amendment legalises it.
- **Typical field set** across cantons (superset of the federal list): establishment name; surname and first name; date of birth; place of birth (foreigners) or Heimatort (Swiss); nationality; exact home address; accompanying spouse/children and number of family members; profession; means of transport and vehicle plate; arriving from; arrival date; departure date; destination after departure; signature; type of identity document; document number. [F6, section 3.2.2]
- **Systems** (study table 5): Basel-Stadt uses **eLM/AVS** (vendor AVS GmbH, Bayreuth); Zürich uses a web portal based on **HOKO** (Unisys, Polis family); Geneva, Vaud, Fribourg, Jura, Neuchâtel use **CheckIn** (OmniSoftory Engineering SA); Ticino uses a **cantonal portal of the Centro Sistemi Informativi**. [F6, section 3.3.7]
- **Formats named in the study**: Basel-Stadt eLM accepts (1) a reservation-system interface, (2) upload of an **XML file**, (3) manual entry. HOKO accepts electronic delivery via interfaces to hotel management systems, or web front end / **Excel upload**. CheckIn accepts manual entry, file upload or a direct HMS interface. Ticino accepts **XML or CSV** export from the HMS uploaded to the portal, or manual entry. [F6, sections 3.4.1 to 3.4.4] The study does not publish the schemas.
- **Data use beyond police**: where transmission is electronic, data also serve tourist-tax billing, guest cards and statistics, *"in anonymisierter und reduzierter Form"*. [F6, sections 3.3.6, 3.3.7]


### 3.1 Zürich (ZH)

- **Who**: all guests, Swiss and foreign. PolG (LS 550.1, version in force 1 Jan 2026) § 21 Abs. 4: *"Die Beherbergungsbetriebe führen eine Gästekontrolle und stellen Meldescheine aus. Diese sind der Polizei zum Zweck der Identitätsfeststellung zur Verfügung zu stellen."* (Accommodation establishments keep a guest register and issue registration forms. These are to be made available to the police for the purpose of establishing identity.) The provision does not distinguish by nationality. [ZH1] Survey answer "Swiss guests also recorded: yes". [F6]
- **Police use**: § 21 Abs. 5 allows the police to retrieve the identification data from the forms electronically and check them *"systematisch und automatisiert"* against search systems. [ZH1]
- **Fields**: POLIS-Verordnung (LS 551.103, version in force 1 Jan 2022) § 7b, "Inhalt der Gästekontrolle": *"a. Name, Vorname, b. Geschlecht, c. Geburtsdatum, d. Nationalität, e. Adresse, f. Ausweispapier (bei ausländischer Staatsangehörigkeit), g. Ausweisnummer (bei ausländischer Staatsangehörigkeit), h. Aufenthaltsdaten, i. Fahrzeugdaten, j. Bemerkungen (z. B. Gruppenbuchungen)."* (surname, first name; sex; date of birth; nationality; address; identity document and document number for foreign nationals only; stay data; vehicle data; remarks such as group bookings). The wording is *"kann ... enthalten"* (may contain), so it is the permitted maximum of the police data set, not a list of mandatory inputs. Which of these the portal enforces as mandatory: **UNVERIFIED** (portal is behind a login). [ZH2]
- **Signature**: neither PolG § 21 nor the POLIS-Verordnung mentions a signature. [ZH1][ZH2] For foreign guests the federal signature rule (VZAE Art. 18) applies on top. For Swiss guests no signature requirement was found in Zürich law. The federal study describes the Zürich practice from the guest's view: a guest arriving in Zürich *"erwartet ... ein Dokument zur Unterschrift. Dieses erhält er jedoch nicht, da seine Daten aufgrund der Buchung bereits im System vorhanden sind"* (expects a document to sign but does not get one, because the data are already in the system). [F6, section 3.3.9] No Zürich police publication stating that the signature may be omitted was retrieved: **UNVERIFIED as an official position**.
- **Transmission**: electronic and mandatory according to the Stadtpolizei Zürich leaflet: *"Entsprechende Meldungen sind elektronisch vorzunehmen. Die notwendigen Anmeldeformulare können bei der Kantonspolizei Zürich über hotelkontrolle@kapo.zh.ch bezogen werden."* (Reports are to be made electronically. Enrolment forms are available from the Kantonspolizei.) [ZH3] Portal: `https://www.hotelkontrolle.zh.ch` (redirects to a Kantonspolizei login, confirmed 2026-09-29). [ZH4]
- **System and formats**: HOKO (Hotelkontrolle), Unisys, part of the Polis family run by Arge Polis. Features listed by Arge Polis: *"Schnittstellen zur Hotelsoftware (keine Mehrfacherfassung); Web-Frontend bzw. Excel-Upload zur manuellen Datenerfassung (kleinere Betriebe); Geschützte Datenübermittlung SiDAP"*; 900,000 forms per year; 130 establishments connected by interface. The vendor flyer (March 2024) names Protel, Micros-Fidelio and Amadeus as supported hotel systems. [ZH5][ZH6] **The interface specification (file layout, transport, authentication) is not public; it must be requested from the Kantonspolizei Zürich / Unisys. UNVERIFIED.**
- **Retention**: on the police side, POLIS-Verordnung § 18 Abs. 5 lit. r: *"Daten der Gästekontrolle 3 Jahre"* (guest register data are deleted after 3 years). [ZH2] A retention period for the copy held by the hotel was not found in PolG or the POLIS-Verordnung: **UNVERIFIED**.

### 3.2 Bern (BE)

- **Who**: all overnight guests of a hospitality establishment. GGG (BSG 935.11, Stand 1 Aug 2024) Art. 24 Abs. 1: *"Über die in einem Gastgewerbebetrieb übernachtenden Gäste ist zu sicherheitspolizeilichen Zwecken eine Kontrolle gemäss den Weisungen der Wirtschafts-, Energie- und Umweltdirektion zu führen."* (A register of guests staying overnight in a hospitality establishment is to be kept for security-police purposes according to the directives of the Economic, Energy and Environment Directorate.) The article does not distinguish by nationality. Abs. 3: *"Im Übrigen gilt Artikel 129 des Polizeigesetzes ... Die eidgenössischen Vorschriften über die Anmeldung von Ausländerinnen und Ausländern bleiben vorbehalten."* (federal rules on foreign nationals are reserved). [BE1]
  - Conflict to note: in the federal survey Bern did **not** tick "Swiss guests also recorded". [F6] The statute text covers all guests; the survey answer probably reflects that nothing is transmitted to the police for Swiss guests. Treat the statute as controlling: keep a record for every guest.
- **Fields**: PolV (BSG 551.111, Stand 1 Aug 2024) Art. 58 Abs. 1: *"Gästedaten gemäss Artikel 129 PolG umfassen a Name und Vorname gemäss Ausweispapier, b Geschlecht, c Wohnadresse, d Nationalität, e Ausweispapier bei ausländischen Personen, f Name des Beherbergungsbetriebs, g An- und Abreisedatum."* (surname and first name as in the identity document; sex; home address; nationality; identity document for foreign persons; establishment name; arrival and departure dates). [BE3] Date of birth is not on the cantonal list, but it is on the federal list for foreign guests (section 1.3).
- **Signature**: not mentioned in GGG Art. 24, PolG Art. 129 or PolV Art. 58. [BE1][BE2][BE3] The directives (Weisungen) of the Directorate that Art. 24 refers to could not be retrieved. A search-engine excerpt of a former cantonal knowledge-base page (vol.be.ch, no longer online, not archived) reads *"Eine Unterschrift der Gäste ist nicht erforderlich, weshalb eine vollständig elektronische Anmeldung möglich ist."* (A guest signature is not required, so a fully electronic registration is possible.) This would apply to the cantonal register only; the federal signature rule for foreign guests is reserved by Art. 24 Abs. 3. **UNVERIFIED** (source page not retrievable after two attempts). A secondary source (software vendor FAQ) reproduces the directive as saying that for several persons travelling together the data of one person with the number of companions suffice. **UNVERIFIED**. [BE4]
- **Transmission**: no routine transmission. The police inspect on demand: PolG (BSG 551.1, Stand 1 Sep 2026) Art. 129 Abs. 1: *"Die Kantonspolizei kann von den Beherbergungsbetrieben die Einsichtnahme in die Gästedaten verlangen."* Abs. 2: where guest data are recorded electronically, the police may *"die zur Identifikation von Personen erforderlichen Angaben anfordern und elektronisch abrufen sowie automatisiert in den polizeilichen Systemen überprüfen"* (request and retrieve them electronically and check them automatically). [BE2] PolV Art. 58 Abs. 2: *"Die Kantonspolizei kann den Beherbergungsbetrieben eine elektronische Plattform zur Übermittlung der angeforderten Gästedaten zur Verfügung stellen."* (may provide an electronic platform). [BE3] The survey lists Bern as "digital system planned", "forms inspected on demand". [F6] Whether the platform exists today and its format: **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Retention**: hotel side **at least five years**: GGG Art. 24 Abs. 2: *"Die Unterlagen sind mindestens fünf Jahre geordnet aufzubewahren und den Kontrollorganen jederzeit zur Einsichtnahme zur Verfügung zu stellen."* [BE1] Police side: destroyed *"nach längstens fünf Jahren"* (after five years at most), PolV Art. 58 Abs. 3. [BE3]

### 3.3 Luzern (LU)

- **Who**: establishments requiring a licence register **Swiss and foreign** guests; establishments without licence requirement register foreign guests under federal law. GaG (SRL 980, Stand 1 May 2026) § 20 Abs. 1: *"Wer einen bewilligungspflichtigen Beherbergungsbetrieb führt, hat eine wahrheitsgetreue Gästekontrolle zu führen."* (Whoever runs an accommodation establishment requiring a licence must keep a truthful guest register.) [LU1] Luzerner Polizei: *"Die Verpflichtung, eine Gästekontrolle zu führen bezieht sich bei bewilligungspflichtigen Betrieben auf in- und ausländische Gäste."* and, for the federal duty, *"Diese Verpflichtung gilt auch für nicht bewilligungspflichtige Betriebe."* [LU3]
- **Fields**: no statutory list in § 20 GaG or § 22a of the ordinance. The police publish the form to use (Stand June 2025), which is the four-language Swiss standard form: establishment; surname; first name; date of birth; place of birth (for Swiss citizens: Heimatort); nationality; full home address; accompanied by wife/children and number of family members; profession; means of transport (public/private) and vehicle plate; coming from; date of departure; destination; signature; and, to be filled in by the host for foreigners, type of identity document and number. [LU4] The form has no labelled arrival-date field in the extracted text; arrival date is a federal mandatory field (section 1.3), so record it regardless.
- **Signature**: required, per the police page quoting federal law: foreign guests' form is to be *"von der beherbergten Person unterschreiben zu lassen"*. The published form has a signature field for every guest. [LU3][LU4] No Luzern text permitting an electronic signature or waiving the signature was found. Tablet signature: **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Groups**: *"Die Reiseleitung füllt den Hotelmeldeschein für sich persönlich komplett aus und legt eine separate Liste der Reisegruppe bei. ... Auf der Liste müssen die folgenden Daten der Reisenden aufgeführt sein: Name, Vorname, Geburtsdatum, Nationalität"* (tour leader completes a full form for themselves and attaches a list with surname, first name, date of birth, nationality of each traveller). [LU3]
- **Transmission**: no routine transmission; forms are kept and produced on request. GaG § 20 Abs. 2: *"Der Meldeschein ist den Polizeiorganen zur Verfügung zu stellen."* Ordinance § 22a Abs. 2: *"Sie sind der Luzerner Polizei auf Verlangen zur Verfügung zu stellen."* [LU1][LU2]
- **New since 1 Jan 2026**: GaG § 20 Abs. 3 (inserted 20 Oct 2025, in force 1 Jan 2026): *"Zur Erhebung der Daten können die Beherbergenden eine elektronische Plattform verwenden. Der Regierungsrat kann die Verwendung der Plattform zur Erhebung der Tourismusabgaben für die Datenerhebung des Meldescheines für obligatorisch erklären."* (Providers may use an electronic platform to collect the data. The government may declare use of the tourist-levy platform mandatory for collecting the registration data.) [LU1] Whether the government has made it mandatory, and which platform and format it is: **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Retention**: **five years**, paper or digital. Ordinance (SRL 981) § 22a Abs. 1: *"Die Meldescheine der Gästekontrolle sind durch die Beherbergungsbetriebe während fünf Jahren aufzubewahren."* [LU2] Police page: *"Die Aufbewahrung kann in Papierform oder digital erfolgen."* (Storage may be on paper or digital.) [LU3]

### 3.4 Basel-Stadt (BS)

- **Who**: all guests. Gastgewerbegesetz (SG 563.100, Stand 25 Aug 2022) § 35 Abs. 1 (wording in force since 25 Aug 2022): *"Die Beherbergungsbetriebe sind verpflichtet, die Meldedaten ihrer Gäste vollständig und wahrheitsgetreu täglich im dafür vorgesehenen Meldesystem zu erfassen."* (Accommodation establishments are obliged to record the registration data of their guests completely and truthfully, daily, in the reporting system provided for that purpose.) No distinction by nationality. [BS1] Survey: Swiss guests recorded: yes. [F6]
- **Timing**: **daily** entry is a statutory requirement. [BS1]
- **Fields**: not listed in § 35; the ordinance to the Gastgewerbegesetz (SG 563.110, Stand 6 Feb 2020) contains no provision on guest registration (full-text search for Gästekontrolle, Meldeschein, Meldedaten returned nothing). [BS2] The field set is defined by the eLM system itself. **Exact mandatory field list: UNVERIFIED** (system documentation not public).
- **Signature**: § 35 contains no signature requirement. [BS1] The statute is built around entry in an electronic system, and the federal study states that cantons with electronic reporting already waive the signature. [F6, section 3.3.5] For foreign guests, VZAE Art. 18 still formally requires one until amended. No Basel-Stadt publication addressing the signature was retrieved: **UNVERIFIED**.
- **System**: **eLM (elektronisches Logiernächtemanagement)**, in operation since October 2021, vendor AVS GmbH. One entry serves four reports: *"Meldung von Daten für die Gasttaxenabrechnung an das Amt für Wirtschaft und Arbeit; Meldung von Personendaten an die Kantonspolizei (Fahndung); Meldung von statistischen Angaben an das Statistische Amt (Tourismusstatistik); Meldung von Daten für den Ausdruck der BaselCard an Basel Tourismus."* [BS3]

### 3.5 Graubünden (GR)

[GR2] Official form content: **UNVERIFIED** (form not retrieved); the federal mandatory list in section 1.3 applies in any case.

- **Signature**: **required, by cantonal ordinance**, and the guest fills in the form: Art. 9 Abs. 1: *"Die beherbergende Person sorgt dafür, dass der Meldeschein von der beherbergten Person ordnungsgemäss ausgefüllt und unterzeichnet wird."* (The host ensures that the form is duly filled in and signed by the guest.) Form of signature not specified. This cantonal signature rule will remain in force after a federal amendment unless Graubünden amends it. [GR2]
- **ID check**: statutory: *"Die Angaben sind anhand eines gültigen Identitätsausweises (Pass, Personalausweis oder anderes Passersatzpapier) zu überprüfen"* (the details must be checked against a valid identity document). Art. 9 Abs. 2. [GR2]

[GR2] The survey marks "electronic transmission possible: yes", "forms only inspected on demand: yes", "digital system in use: no". [F6] Channel and format for the electronic option: **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Retention**: **one year**. Art. 10 Abs. 1: *"Die beherbergende Person hat die Meldescheine während eines Jahres aufzubewahren und sie den Polizeiorganen jederzeit zur Verfügung zu stellen."* [GR2]

### 3.7 Tessin / Ticino (TI)

Mountain huts and group lodgings under LEAR Art. 3 cpv. 1 lit. g are exempt (Art. 2 cpv. 2). [TI1]
  - Conflict to note: in the federal survey Ticino did not tick "Swiss guests also recorded". [F6] The regulation of 2024 says "ogni ospite" and the import format lists Switzerland (CHE) as an example nationality value. [TI1][TI3] Treat the regulation as controlling: notify all guests.
- **Deadline**: **24 hours** from arrival (Art. 2 cpv. 1). [TI1]
- **Transmission**: **electronic is mandatory**. Art. 3 cpv. 1: *"Le notifiche sono trasmesse al Servizio in forma elettronica tramite l'apposita piattaforma su internet."* (Notifications are transmitted to the Service electronically through the dedicated internet platform.) Paper only exceptionally, on written reasoned request, using pads supplied by the Service (Art. 3 cpv. 2). [TI1] Platform: `https://www.notifiche.ti.ch`, login with a one-time code from an authenticator app. [TI4]

In addition Art. 6 cpv. 2: *"Viene registrato anche il numero e il genere del documento, senza procedere alla fotocopia dello stesso."* (The number and type of the document are also recorded, **without photocopying it**.) [TI1]
- **ID check**: statutory for all guests. Art. 4 cpv. 1: *"tutti gli ospiti devono essere registrati sulla base di un documento d'identità valido"*; Art. 6 cpv. 1: guests hand over a valid identity document, which is returned once the data are recorded. [TI1] **No copy or scan of the document may be kept** (Art. 6 cpv. 2). This is a hard product constraint.
- **Signature**: the regulation contains **no signature requirement**. [TI1] The import format has no signature element. [TI3] For foreign guests VZAE Art. 18 formally still requires a signature until amended; the federal report names Ticino as a canton with an established digital system, and the federal study says such cantons already waive the signature. [F4][F6] No Ticino police statement on the point was retrieved: **practice UNVERIFIED**.
- **Families**: *"Le famiglie (marito, moglie, partner registrato e figli maggiorenni) sono notificate separatamente con una notifica per persona. I figli minori di 18 anni vengono notificati con uno dei genitori."* (Spouses, registered partners and adult children are each notified separately; children under 18 are notified with one parent.) Art. 4 cpv. 2. [TI1] This differs from Graubünden (one form per couple) and from the federal "accompanying spouse" field.
- **Groups**: minimum 6 persons including the leader. Record the leader's personal data with the number of members, split into over and under 14 years. Art. 5. [TI1]

- **Retention**: 5 years for group lists and parental authorisations (above). No retention period is set for the individual notification on the provider's side, since it lives in the police platform. Provider-side retention of the individual record: **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Import formats (published)**: the police page "Come posso importare i pernottamenti già inseriti nel mio gestionale (PMS) nel portale Notifiche di Polizia?" offers three models: Excel (`.xlsx`), CSV and XML, with an XSD. ## 4. Tourist tax and guest card where they touch registration

General pattern: levies are cantonal and/or communal, charged per person per night, with exemptions by age and residence. The registration record is the natural source for the night count, the exemption decision (date of birth, place of residence) and the guest card. Product consequences:

- Tax rules need **age bands with different thresholds per place** (under 6, 6 to 16 half rate, under 12, under 16, over 16) and a **residence test** (resident of the commune, of the canton, tax domicile). Date of birth and place of residence must therefore be captured for every guest, including children, even where the police rule does not require a separate form for them.
- Rates are per person per night, fixed amounts, per commune or canton, sometimes by season and accommodation category: a configurable table per property, as already decided for Germany.
- Guest-card issuing is a separate integration per destination (BaselCard via eLM, Ticino Ticket via Cariboo, destination systems elsewhere).

## 5. Requirements for a Switzerland mode

### A. Certain (statute or official text in force, quoted above)

1. **Jurisdiction is the canton, not the country.** The property's canton selects the rule set: who is registered, fields, signature, transmission channel, deadline, retention. A single "Switzerland" rule set is wrong. [F4][F6]
2. **Foreign guests are always registered**, in every canton, under federal law, with the federal mandatory fields: surname, first name, date of birth, place of birth, nationality, accompanying spouse and minor children, arrival date, departure date, type and number of identity document, establishment name. [F1][F2][F4]
3. **Swiss guests are registered in ZH, BE, LU (licensed establishments), VS, GE, TI and BS; not in GR.** [ZH1][BE1][LU1][VS1][GE1][TI1][BS1][GR2]
4. **The ID Check is mandatory and already fits the ticket 11 flow**: the guest presents an identity document and the host compares the data (VZAE Art. 18; GR Art. 9; VS Art. 15; GE Art. 32 al. 2; TI Art. 4 and 6). [F2][GR2][VS1][GE1][TI1]
5. **Never store a copy or scan of the identity document for Ticino properties** (RL 942.120 Art. 6 cpv. 2); record type and number only. Safest default for all Swiss properties. [TI1]
6. **Until the VZAE amendment enters into force, the registration of a foreign guest needs the guest's signature** (VZAE Art. 18 Abs. 1 in force). Keep the printed form with handwritten signature available in every canton as the path that is certainly compliant. [F2]
7. **Graubünden requires the signature by its own ordinance** (Art. 9 Abs. 1), and the guest, not the host, fills in the form. This stays after a federal change unless the canton amends it. [GR2]
8. **Group path**: a list signed by the tour leader (federal); cantonal variants: GR collective list from more than 5 persons plus a form for the leader; TI group from 6 persons, leader's data plus member counts over/under 14 and a member list kept 5 years; LU leader's full form plus list with surname, first name, date of birth, nationality; VS leader registers plus list of names. [F2][GR2][TI1][LU3][VS1]
9. **Family handling differs**: federal and LU form record accompanying spouse and children on one form; GR one form per married or registered couple, children on a guardian's form; TI one notification per adult (spouses and adult children separately), minors under 18 with one parent. The form-per-guest decision must be a per-canton rule. [F4][LU4][GR2][TI1]
10. **Transmission deadlines**: TI within 24 hours of arrival; GE daily arrival files; BS daily entry; ZH electronic (deadline not found). BE, GR, LU, VS: no routine transmission, keep and produce on request. [TI1][GE1][BS1][ZH3][BE2][GR2][LU2][VS1]
11. **Retention on the provider side, per canton**: BE at least 5 years; LU 5 years (paper or digital); GR 1 year; GE current year until end of the following January; TI 5 years for group lists and parental authorisations. Deletion must be configurable per canton. [BE1][LU2][GR2][GE1][TI1]
12. **Ticino file export** can be built now against a published format: XML (with XSD), CSV or XLSX, dates as dates, nationality and country as ISO 3166-1 alpha-3, document type from a fixed list, first field the establishment's user id or LEAR number; uploaded by a logged-in user, per-row result. [TI3][TI5]
13. **Unaccompanied minors in Ticino**: capture and keep a written parental authorisation for 5 years; flag to staff that the police must be informed if it is missing. [TI1]
14. **Tourist tax needs date of birth and residence for every guest**, with per-place age bands and exemptions, as a configurable per-property table. [BS5][LU5][VS3][BE5][GE4]
15. **The German card-payment path must not be offered in Switzerland.** It has no basis in Swiss law and the federal report rejects it as impractical. [F4]

### B. Unsettled (do not hard-code; build as configuration)

16. **Whether a signature drawn on a tablet satisfies VZAE Art. 18 today.** No federal or cantonal text found decides it. The federal study says cantons with electronic reporting already waive the signature and that cantonal practice may deviate from the handwritten-signature rule; the federal report notes online signatures are already requested in practice. Recommended default: capture the tablet signature (it costs little, mirrors Austria mode, and is stronger evidence than none), and let the property switch to "printed and hand-signed" per canton. **Confirm with each cantonal police before relying on it.** [F4][F6]
17. **Whether and when the amendment is adopted, and with what final text.** Consultation ends 27 Nov 2026; no entry-into-force date. If adopted as drafted: electronic form without signature, provided the host checks the data against the presented document; paper form still signed; groups electronic without signature if the tour leader's contact details are included. Build the signature step as a per-canton switch that can be turned off by configuration, with the ID Check remaining mandatory. [F3][F4][F5]
18. **Cantonal signature rules after the federal change.** GR has its own signature rule; LU publishes a form with a signature field for all guests. Whether cantons follow the federal change is open. [GR2][LU4]
19. **EasyGov channel**: planned for 2028, voluntary, portal only. No API or data format published. Do not plan against it yet. [F4]
20. **Interface specifications for HOKO (ZH), eLM (BS) and CheckIn (GE)** are not public. Each needs a request to the operator (Kantonspolizei Zürich / Unisys; Amt für Wirtschaft und Arbeit Basel-Stadt / AVS; Geneva police / OmniSoftory). The federal report states that no standard exists. Until obtained, the fallback per canton is manual entry in the cantonal portal, supported by an export screen or file. [F4][F6][ZH5][BS6]
21. **Mandatory field lists in ZH, BS, GE, VS** are defined by the system or the police-approved form, not by statute. Capture the superset (federal list plus sex, home address, vehicle plate where given) and validate per canton once the specifications are obtained. [ZH2][BS1][GE1][VS1]
22. **Wallis form approval**: a PMS-generated arrival form must be *"von der Kantonspolizei genehmigt"*. Procedure not found. [VS1]
23. **Luzern platform**: permitted since 1 Jan 2026, may be declared mandatory by the government; status and format unknown. [LU1]
24. **Bern directive** of the Wirtschafts-, Energie- und Umweltdirektion (content of the register, signature, companions) could not be retrieved; obtain it before building Bern specifics. [BE1]
25. **Provider-side retention in ZH, BS, VS** not found in the statutes read. Until clarified, a conservative configurable default is needed; data-protection law (revised FADP) limits keeping data longer than necessary, which was not researched here.
26. **Conflicts between the 2024 survey and statute text** for Bern and Ticino (Swiss guests) and Geneva (existence of a cantonal rule) were resolved in favour of the statute text; confirm with the cantonal police on first customer in each canton. [F6][BE1][TI1][GE1]

### C. Suggested build order (opinion, not a finding)

Cantonal rule table first (who, fields, signature switch, family rule, group rule, deadline, retention); printed form with signature as universal path; tablet signature as configurable default; Ticino file export as the first electronic channel because its format is published; ZH, BS and GE channels after obtaining specifications.
