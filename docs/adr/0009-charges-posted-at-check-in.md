# All nights are posted to the folio at check-in, each charge carrying a service date

At check-in the system posts one charge per night per component (room, breakfast, city tax) for the whole stay, each with the service date of its night. Most property management systems post each night during the night audit; the product owner chose posting at check-in so the guest and the front desk see the full bill from the first minute and an invoice can be issued at any time.

## Consequences

- Revenue and tax reports use the service date, never the posting date.
- Early departure voids future-dated charges; extension posts the added nights immediately.
- The night audit does not post room charges; it verifies them.
- An invoice issued before departure contains future service dates.
