# Card provider confirmation of card import

Map: ../map.md
Type: task
Status: open
Blocked by: -

## Question

"Cards and channel bookings at the switch" offers card transfer from a hotel's old provider only if our card provider confirms that stored cards can be imported into a connected account of a hotel on our platform. HITL: the owner, or a named person, asks the card provider in writing: is import into connected accounts supported, who files the request (platform or hotel), what proof of the sending provider's certification is needed, how long it takes, and whether imported cards can be charged later without the guest present. Record the answer; it switches the card transfer on or off.
