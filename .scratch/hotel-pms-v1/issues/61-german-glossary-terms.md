## Question

"Staff app languages and time zones" decided that every glossary term gets exactly one German word taken from hotel trade usage. Draw up the list with the owner: for each term in `CONTEXT.md` propose the German word German hotels use (for example Night Audit as Tagesabschluss, Held Rooms as Kontingent, Room Rack as Zimmerspiegel, Cash Book as Kassenbuch), mark where Austrian or Swiss usage differs, and where the English term itself stays in German screens (Folio, Check-in). The owner confirms each word. Record the result as a German column in the glossary.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-09-30 with the owner. The German word for every one of the 128 glossary terms is in `docs/glossary/german-terms.md`, which is now the reference for screens, help and support; `CONTEXT.md` links to it rather than repeating the column.

Chosen by the owner among alternatives: Night Audit = Tagesabschluss; Business Date = **Hoteltag** (owner's own word); Tenant = Mandant; Rate Plan = Rate; Rate = Tagespreis; Held Rooms = Kontingent; Tentative = Option; Section = Revier; Out of Order and Out of Service stay in English; Guest Inbox = Posteingang. All other rows accepted as proposed. Austrian and Swiss variants noted where they differ (Meldeschein/Gästeblatt, City Tax by place).

New glossary terms from later tickets must get a German word in the same file when they are added.
