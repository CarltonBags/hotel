# Map: Hotel PMS v1

Label: wayfinder:map
Effort: hotel-pms-v1
Tickets: `.scratch/hotel-pms-v1/issues/`

## Destination

A **buildable v1 spec** for a multi-tenant SaaS hotel PMS. Tenant = hotel company owning 1..N properties, each up to 400 rooms, EU-wide with DACH first. Surfaces: staff PMS (reservations, front desk, housekeeping, rates/inventory, folio/billing, reporting, multi-property admin), guest portal (self check-in/out, messaging, spa booking), own point of sale for bar, restaurant and spa, channel sync via one third-party channel manager. The spec consists of the domain glossary in `CONTEXT.md`, the tech stack, the data model, the UI/UX direction, a module map, and ADRs. Done when `/to-tickets` can run against it with nothing left to decide.

## Notes

- Domain: hotel operations. Consult `CONTEXT.md` before naming any concept; call `domain-modeling` whenever a ticket resolves a term.
- Default ticket skills: `grilling` + `domain-modeling`. Prototypes: `prototype` (UI branch for screens). Research: `research`, findings saved to `docs/research/<slug>.md`.
- Tracker: local markdown, see `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md`.
- Standing decisions from charting (not tickets):
  - Stack: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Postgres. Details are decided in "Tech stack detail and tenancy strategy".
  - Markets: EU-wide, Germany/DACH first. Per-property legal and tax config is required.
  - Guest side is in scope: booking engine, guest portal, self check-in.
  - Channel connectivity via one third-party channel manager, never direct OTA APIs.
  - Shell decided in "App shell and multi-tab navigation prototype"; every later screen prototype is built inside that shell (`apps/staff-shell-prototype`, variant E).
  - Guest-facing surfaces (guest portal, self check-in) share the cinematic look decided in "Self check-in guest interface" and "Booking engine look prototype".
  - Design direction: "super modern" look in the spirit of Warmwind OS. Click-first navigation, not keyboard-first, but every data-heavy form must be fully keyboard-operable. Multi-tab workspace inside one browser session (open/close tabs easily). Light and dark mode. Meaningful icons where they aid recognition. Self check-in is a separate, Apple-like, guest-facing interface.
  - Meldeschein (guest registration form) is completed on a guest-facing iPad pushed from the front desk. Charting assumed a tablet signature; the DACH research shows that is valid in Austria but not Germany. The Meldeschein signing flow ticket carries the corrected rules.

- [Tenancy, property and role model](issues/01-tenancy-property-role-model.md): Tenant owns Legal Entities and Properties; one user per tenant; tenant roles Owner/Tenant Admin imply Property Manager everywhere; five fixed property roles per property; cross-property dashboard, search and reports.
- [Reservation and inventory domain model](issues/02-reservation-inventory-domain.md): availability on Room Type; Booking (Booker, Source) holds Reservations; date-ranged Room Assignments handle room moves; five states, no Tentative; in-place edits with audit log; staff-only overbooking; no-shows proposed by Night Audit with exclusions; tenant-wide Guest profiles.
- [Folio, billing and invoicing domain model](issues/10-folio-billing-domain.md): 1..N Folios per Reservation, one Bill-to each; all nights posted at check-in as per-night Charges with Service Date; gross stored with Tax Code; City Tax separate line; automatic Deposit Invoice; Card Holds tracked; Void before invoice, Cancellation Invoice after; gap-free numbering per Legal Entity; ZUGFeRD PDFs; Receivables with manual matching.
- [Housekeeping model](issues/13-housekeeping-model.md): room state in three dimensions (Cleanliness, derived Occupancy, Room Block); Out of Order removes from sale, Out of Service only flags; tasks generated after Night Audit and distributed by Section; roles grow to seven (Housekeeper, Housekeeping Supervisor, Maintenance); phone-first view for floor staff; check-in to a non-ready room needs override; minibar, lost and found, declined cleaning in v1.
- [Booking engine flow](issues/14-booking-engine-flow.md): hosted page per property under the hotel's domain plus search widget; tenant page across hotels; six-step flow; total price including VAT with City Tax line; guarantee from the rate plan's Payment Policy; 10-minute Inventory Hold; up to 5 rooms per checkout; Rate Codes unlock hidden rate plans; self-service cancel, date change and extras.
- [Rates and restrictions model](issues/15-rates-restrictions-model.md): Rate Plan spans room types; Rate per plan, room type and date in a daily grid; base plans and one-level derived plans stored as daily values; base occupancy plus Supplements with Age Bands; six Restrictions; included Services with fixed component prices; policies on the plan; reservations keep their prices; Price Override with Price Floor.
- [Permission matrix for fixed roles](issues/16-permission-matrix.md): full matrix for seven roles; several roles per user add up; Approval by Property Manager on the spot or remotely; Front Desk refunds up to a property limit; Front Desk may cancel invoices and set any Cleanliness; floor staff get the Floor View; erasure requested by Property Manager and executed at tenant level.
- [Night audit and business date](issues/17-night-audit-business-date.md): Business Date per property, advanced only by a staff-started audit; missing arrivals decided one by one (No-show with fee, or Late Arrival); only undecided arrivals and overdue departures block; close is all-or-nothing; closed days are fixed and corrected by new entries; missed days are caught up in order; report stored, not emailed.
- [Cash handling and fiscal cash-register obligations](issues/18-cash-and-tse.md): cash in v1 behind a cloud fiscal module, switchable per property; Cash Registers with Shifts, blind count and difference booking; Paid-outs, deposits, tips; value Vouchers sold at desk and online, redeemed at desk within one Legal Entity; Receipts electronic by default; desk keeps working during fiscal outage.
- [Guest inbox and messaging model](issues/19-guest-inbox-messaging.md): one Conversation per reservation with earlier stays visible; shared Guest Inbox per property with optional claim; automated messages in the same thread; guest may reply by email; quick replies and Internal Notes; images and PDF both ways; badge, toast, escalation after 15 minutes; Desk Hours reply; deleted 12 months after departure; no translation in v1.
- [Calendar (room plan) screen prototype](issues/22-calendar-screen-prototype.md): Rooms view as main (rows per room grouped by type, unassigned lane, bars from midday to midday, blocks drawn in rows) plus Availability view as second; room change at once with undo, date or type change confirmed first; filter bar for large hotels; "All properties" shows availability per hotel.
- [Fiscal service provider selection](issues/30-fiscal-service-provider-selection.md): fiskaly for Germany and Austria under one account, efsta as fallback; nothing installed at the hotel; Germany signs every receipt-producing desk process, Austria only on-site payments; provider holds data three months so the product archives exports; prices unpublished, quotation needed.
- [City tax rule configuration](issues/24-city-tax-rule-configuration.md): one dated City Tax Rule per property, of type percentage, step table or flat; Rates exclude the tax; charged on top or absorbed per property; channel prices that already contain the tax are split backwards; exemptions from a fixed list with evidence; filing report per period; presets for Berlin, Hamburg, Wien.
- [Housekeeper and maintenance phone view prototype](issues/27-housekeeper-phone-view-prototype.md): list by Section with a bottom room sheet (Start, Done, declined, minibar, problem, lost and found); supervisor board with Publish and live changes; inspection on phone and desktop; maintenance queue ends Room Blocks; Housekeeping Notes from a fixed list; ten phone languages per user; phone view works offline and sends later.
- [Booking engine look prototype](issues/28-booking-engine-look-prototype.md): cinematic look with photo hero, photo cards per room type and floating cart bar; hotel chooses dark or light; page takes logo, photos, accent colour and a font pairing; up to three rate plans shown per room type; room detail sheet; summary sheet from the cart bar; chain page, website widget and voucher page in the same look.
- [Rates grid screen prototype](issues/29-rates-grid-prototype.md): spreadsheet grid with keyboard entry; derived rows read-only under their base; bulk edit panel with preview before applying; changes saved at once with undo and sent to channels; prices below floor saved with warning; tools for paste, copy period, fill to horizon and Event Markers.
- [Reporting scope](issues/32-reporting-scope.md): nine fixed reports; Available Rooms exclude Out of Order; rate figures on Net Room Revenue by Service Date; no-show fees in their own line; comparisons with previous period, last year and Budget; table-only report builder following role rights; export, scheduling, raw data export.
- [Email, SMS and WhatsApp delivery providers](issues/44-message-delivery-provider-research.md): email through Mailgun EU with Amazon SES Frankfurt as fallback; SMS through seven.io; WhatsApp through the platform owner's own interface with each hotel holding its own account; WhatsApp allows free text only for 24 hours after the guest wrote, else approved templates; Austrian SMS sender names need registration from 1 October 2026.
- [Search visibility of hosted booking pages](issues/46-search-visibility-research.md): v1 builds indexable pages with hotel markup and language versions, no price feed; free booking links stay with the channel manager and land on its own booking page until an own account is approved; price shown must equal price sent, taxes included.
- [Door lock systems and their integration](issues/40-door-lock-vendor-research.md): "collect at reception" stays the default; one asynchronous lock interface run by the worker; v1 supports cloud lock systems only, first through an aggregator if its European data handling and pricing are confirmed; on offline locks a card works until its written expiry, so room moves and extensions need the card rewritten.
- [Groups and allotments](issues/33-groups-and-allotments.md): Group as own object with contract; Tentative does not reduce availability, Definite does; Held Rooms return to sale at the Release Date; Series for tour operators; contract prices plus optional Rate Code; Master Folio with group Routing Rules; deposit schedule, fees posted by staff; rooming list import; group check-in screen.
- [Point-of-sale systems and room charge interfaces](issues/42-point-of-sale-research.md): one room charge module with manual posting, one first automatic connection and an own published interface; the till stays the fiscal cash register, the product signs only when the folio is paid; every till charge needs a Tax Code and the till's receipt number; systems needing a component on the hotel network stay out of v1.
- [Switzerland guest registration rules](issues/48-switzerland-registration-research.md): signature still required for foreign guests in every canton, tablet signature unsettled, so printed form is the only certain path; cantons differ in transmission, retention and who is registered; one canton forbids copying identity documents; amendment dropping the signature is in consultation.
- [Austrian and Swiss accounting formats](issues/50-accounting-formats-research.md): keep German format with an Austrian profile plus the neutral CSV; native Austrian and Swiss writers only where a pilot hotel exists; export must carry net, tax and gross per line and a short numeric document number; German chart templates do not fit Austria or Switzerland; card payouts stay unbooked.
- [Tourism statistics reporting duties](issues/53-tourism-statistics-research.md): monthly arrivals and overnight stays per person by country of residence plus capacity figures, mandatory in all three countries; country of residence must be captured for every guest as its own field beside nationality; report ships as screen, spreadsheet and PDF in official form order, no direct transmission in v1.
- [Front desk without internet](issues/34-front-desk-without-internet.md): read-only lists on the device, no changes offline; Downtime Reports printable any time (arrivals, expected departures, in-house, Room Rack); emergency report saved to devices every 2 hours; no payments in the product while offline; Catch-up Entry with real time afterwards; banner, status page and notification.
- [How a hotel's existing website booking tool delivers reservations](issues/56-foreign-booking-tools-research.md): foreign tools connect in v1 only as channels of the channel manager; none of the eleven named tools has a verified connection there; VAT and City Tax inclusion must be a setting per channel; money taken elsewhere appears as recorded Payment; comparison sites are not channels.
- [Shared-device login for floor staff](issues/35-shared-device-login.md): shared phones and tablets are enrolled Devices; staff pick their name and enter a personal PIN that works only there; floor and service roles only; users without email created with a first PIN; lock after 2 minutes idle; 5 wrong tries lock the user; Device can be revoked and wiped; own phones can be enrolled for one user.
- [Onboarding and subscription](issues/36-onboarding-and-subscription.md): trial on demo data or on own data in Test Mode; Go-live assisted with a required checklist; priced per room per month with Modules as add-ons; usage billed at cost plus handling; monthly term; non-payment restricts gradually without sudden lockout; exit with full export and deletion after 90 days.
- [Data import from a previous system](issues/37-data-import.md): future and in-house reservations, guests, Companies, open money, rates, and history as daily figures only; never past invoices, registration records, users or card numbers; spreadsheet templates; dry run before import, undo per Import Batch; one fixed switching day without parallel running; cards only by provider-to-provider transfer or stored again by the guest.
- [Notifications model](issues/38-notifications-model.md): guests get email only, from the hotel's own domain once verified; SMS only for urgent manager alerts; ten automatic message types, no room-ready notice, no marketing messages; fixed layout with editable text blocks per language; documents at once, service messages within Sending Hours; delivery failures shown and alerted.
- [Card transfer between providers and re-delivery of existing channel bookings](issues/59-card-transfer-and-channel-redelivery-research.md): card import between providers exists but is unverified for hotels on a platform account and takes weeks; existing channel bookings arrive only on explicit fetch and incomplete; virtual cards of existing bookings come only from each booking site.
- [Staff app languages and time zones](issues/39-staff-languages-time-zones.md): staff app in German and English per user; one fixed German word per glossary term; formats follow the user's language, documents their own; all times in property time, named for guests; nights are calendar dates; hotel's own names in its main language with guest versions per language.
- [Guest messaging extensions](issues/45-messaging-extensions-decisions.md): WhatsApp as Module per property with Messaging Consent, 24-hour window and approved templates for service messages; documents stay email; Unassigned section for unmatched messages; no guest SMS, no translation; message to task; combined inbox across properties.
- [Search visibility decisions](issues/47-search-visibility-decisions.md): owner removed our booking page from the product; Google may be switched on as a channel, landing on the channel manager's page; nothing else of this ticket applies.
- [Door lock systems used by German hotels and covered by established PMS](issues/62-german-lock-market-research.md): Visionline and Salto Space most frequent, then Ambiance, Onity, Hotek; on-premises locks need a small agent on the hotel network or a bought connector; SIHOT has no lock of its own.
- [Digital key and key card handling](issues/41-digital-key-decisions.md): card at desk and kiosk, phone key, door PIN; keys only for Checked-in, valid to checkout time plus grace; automatic revoke at check-out, no-show, cancellation; one lock interface with a Lock Plugin per vendor; on-premises locks through our outbound-only Site Bridge; every vendor in PMS lists at launch; unsupported locks get a plugin built.
- [Switzerland registration flow](issues/49-switzerland-registration-flow.md): Canton Profile per property with presets for eight cantons; printed form with handwritten signature by default, switchable per canton; no card-payment path; Tessin export file, manual entry elsewhere until specs obtained.
- [Own point of sale for bar, restaurant and spa](issues/54-own-point-of-sale.md): Outlets with tables, open bills, printers, room charge within Spending Limit, card, cash, vouchers, tips; spa sale, appointment book and guest self-booking in the portal; Outlet Sale for walk-ins; PIN devices; offline selling with later signing; roles Service, Spa Staff, Outlet Manager; external tills kept via interface.
- [Restaurant and bar charges to the room](issues/43-restaurant-charges-decisions.md): external tills via Lightspeed, our published room charge interface, and FIAS-based tills through the Site Bridge; one Charge per Tax Code per bill with items as detail; same room charge rules as our own point of sale; till stays fiscal register.
- [Connecting a hotel's existing website booking tool](issues/57-foreign-booking-tools-decisions.md): only as a channel of the channel manager, verified per customer at onboarding; tax inclusion set per channel; tool payments recorded, its guarantees not chargeable by us; guests get Portal and service messages, tool sends its own confirmation.
- [Card acceptance on phones and fiscal duties of an outlet point of sale](issues/55-outlet-payments-and-fiscal-research.md): tap to pay on phones in all three countries but not for girocard, so German outlets need a reader; German orders signed per round, bill signed at creation; Austria signs only payment; offline card payments on readers only, with PIN, not girocard.
- [Support and service levels](issues/58-support-and-service-levels.md): chat and email in office hours, phone emergency line 24/7; response times by severity; built for no noticeable outage, promised 99.95 % with credits; standing support access, logged and visible to the Owner.
- [Cards and channel bookings at the switch](issues/60-cards-and-channel-bookings-at-switch.md): card transfer offered once the card provider confirms it, started two months ahead; import is the truth and channel fetch is only matched; virtual cards of existing bookings taken from each site's extranet; unsecured guarantees handled manually.
- [German terms for the glossary](issues/61-german-glossary-terms.md): one German word for each of 128 terms in `docs/glossary/german-terms.md`; owner's picks include Tagesabschluss, Hoteltag, Mandant, Rate, Kontingent, Revier, Posteingang; Out of Order and Out of Service stay English.
- [Permissions for cash, vouchers and outlet roles](issues/66-permissions-cash-vouchers-outlets.md): desk cash rights accepted as proposed; Service and Spa Staff sell, take payment and room-charge within limit, give small discounts up to a set percentage; voids, refunds and over-limit need Outlet Manager; Revenue has no outlet rights.
- [Channel manager selection](issues/03-channel-manager-selection.md): Channex.io for v1 (public API, free staging, white-label pricing); SiteMinder second, DIRS21 later for DACH. Rates model must be Property > Room Type > Rate Plan with delta-only ARI outbox.
- [Payments provider for EU hotels](issues/04-payments-provider-eu.md): Stripe (Connect direct charges + Terminal) behind a provider-agnostic abstraction, Adyen as enterprise fallback. Card holds expire (7 days, 30 with lodging extended auth), incremental auth capped; SEPA is never a hold; OTA virtual cards are MOTO.
- [DACH legal and fiscal requirements](issues/05-dach-compliance-research.md): German tablet signature on Meldeschein is NOT valid (BMG §29); only handwritten, card-SCA or eID substitutes; Germans need no Meldeschein since 2025; Austria accepts electronic signature. PMS recording cash becomes a TSE-obligated Kassensystem. UStG §14 invoice contents, GoBD immutability, DATEV export shape captured.
- [Guest portal identity model](issues/07-guest-portal-identity.md): no accounts; per-reservation Portal Link + OTP fallback, valid to checkout + 30 days; booker gets booking-level link; registration with ID fields (image opt-in), pay/add card, WhatsApp-style Guest Inbox for staff, online pre-check-in always staff-approved; digital key per-property opt-in.
- [Tech stack detail and tenancy strategy](issues/08-tech-stack-detail.md): schema per tenant + shared control schema, tenant subdomain; Drizzle; Better Auth; Vercel EU + Neon Frankfurt; always-on worker (Postgres queue, SSE realtime, webhook intake); pnpm/Turborepo with apps staff, booking, guest, worker; tablets enrolled as Devices; EU S3-compatible file storage.
- [App shell and multi-tab navigation prototype](issues/09-app-shell-navigation-prototype.md): top navbar with Main Menu button (nested menu of all lists and functions), per-user Quick Access, pinnable tab strip below navbar, one full-width Stage, one browser tab only; light and dark; tenant-chosen accent; bigger and more colourful than round 1.
- [Schema-per-tenant feasibility on Neon with Drizzle](issues/20-schema-per-tenant-feasibility.md): feasible with named constraints; every tenant query inside a transaction that sets the search path first; Drizzle HTTP driver excluded for tenant data; open risk at about 210,000 relations on Neon, load test required before build.
- [Meldeschein tablet signing flow](issues/11-meldeschein-signing-flow.md): one send queues all guests needing a form; tablet steps welcome, details, companions, review, completion; Austria signs on tablet; Germany offers card payment with SCA or printed form with handwritten signature, guest chooses; staff ID Check required to complete; structured record plus PDF, automatic deletion per country.
- [Self check-in guest interface](issues/12-self-checkin-guest-interface.md): cinematic glass look; one flow for lobby kiosk and guest phone; Check-in Conditions tested right after booking found; completes without staff when all hold; room not ready leads to Arrived, Waiting for Room with notification; phone needs presence proof on arrival day; key handover per property setting.
- [Arrival-day card transaction for registration](issues/25-arrival-day-card-transaction.md): card path needs a fresh transaction with strong authentication on arrival day by the guest as cardholder; card hold probably counts, earlier payment does not, zero-amount check does not; ten rules are safe defaults pending a lawyer.
- [Warmwind OS design language](issues/06-warmwind-design-reference.md): adopt left rail + rounded stage shell with in-stage tab strip, ink-alpha colour system mirrored to dark, Lucide icons, light geometric type, 120/280ms motion; wallpaper + glass reserved for self check-in. Satoshi/Circular not free, Inter/Geist fallback.
